TYUST-EmbodiedAI 实验室隶属于太原科技大学，专注于具身智能研究。

The TYUST-EmbodiedAI Lab is affiliated with Taiyuan University of Science and Technology, focusing on embodied intelligence research.

  • 事件相机 Event Camera
